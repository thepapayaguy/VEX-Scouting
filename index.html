Share sheets

Sheets save on this phone only. To combine everyone's scouting, each scout taps Copy my sheets and pastes the code into the team chat. Paste a teammate's code below and tap Add to board. Adding the same code twice is fine; duplicates are skipped.

How the score works

Each sheet scores one team in one match out of 100. The team board averages every sheet for that team, so scout as many of their matches as you can and do not round up.

Auto 20
3 points per auton pin (max 15), +3 clean run or +1 partly worked, +2 if they used 2+ goals without touching the perimeter (an AWP-ready routine).
Score 40
2 points per driver pin (max 24), +2 per cycle-speed level above 1 (max 8), +2 tall goal / +4 top of a tall stack, +2 yellows with toggle control, +1 capping, +1 match loads.

Cycle speed

1
Under one pin per 20 s, or gets stuck often.
2
About one pin per 15–20 s.
3
About one pin per 10–15 s; steady SA middle school level.
4
About one pin per 7–10 s, rarely drops pieces.
5
Under 7 s per pin, picks stacks off the ground; NSW top-team level.

Driver skill

1
Struggles to line up on goals, drives into other robots.
3
Lines up reliably, some wasted movement.
5
Smooth, fast lines, reads the field and avoids defence.
